## Finding leads

Open Leads in the sidebar to see every lead in your organization. Each row shows the lead's name, company, status and score. Use View to open a lead's detail page, with its contact details, dates and its email conversations.

## Lead statuses

A lead moves through these statuses: New, Contacted, Qualified and Lost. Converted means the lead has already become a customer.
