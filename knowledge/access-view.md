## Reading the access list

Settings, Just-in-time Access lists every temporary grant: who has it — a member of the organization, or an email address for someone without an account — which screen, who granted it, and until when. Active grants are working now; expired ones ran out of time; revoked ones were stopped early. An invite that has been used shows when it was redeemed.
