title: Using the assistant
---

## What the assistant can do

The assistant answers questions about your leads, customers, services and figures, and can make changes for you. It only ever sees and does what your own access allows. If you cannot do something in the product, the assistant cannot do it for you either.

## Confirming changes

Before the assistant creates, updates, converts, deletes or sends anything, it shows a "Confirm this change" card describing exactly what it is about to do. Nothing happens until you press Confirm. Press Cancel and nothing is changed. A change you do not decide on within 15 minutes expires.

## Conversation history

Conversations are saved, so reloading the page or signing in from another device continues where you left off. Open the full-page assistant (the expand button on the panel, or Assistant in the sidebar) to see your past conversations on the left. From there you can start a New chat, rename a conversation with the pencil, or delete it with the bin.

## Searching past conversations

On the full-page assistant, type into "Search conversations" above your history. It finds conversations by meaning as well as by exact words, so "pricing talk" can find a conversation about a customer's budget.

## When a message is not answered

If the assistant could not answer, your message stays in the conversation marked "Not answered" with a Retry link. Retrying never asks the same question twice.
