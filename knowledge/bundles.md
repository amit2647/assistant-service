## What a profession bundle is

A profession bundle sets the workspace up for one kind of practice, for example a chartered accountancy firm. It adds the profession's services, role templates, client fields, engagement types, deadline rules, letter templates, portals and reminder emails. Go to Settings, then Profession Bundle, to see the bundles on offer. An organization has one bundle at most.

## Installing and upgrading

Choose Install on the bundle. Each part is set up in turn, and if one part fails, installing again resumes where it stopped. When a newer version is offered, choose Upgrade: the version you have stays in use until every part has finished. Reminder emails a bundle adds are always switched off until you turn them on.

## Your edits are kept

Anything a bundle installs can be edited, and an upgrade never overwrites your edits. Items the newer version no longer ships are retired, not deleted, so existing clients keep them.

## Customized items

When you have edited something that the installed version ships differently, it is listed under Customized items on the Profession Bundle page, with the fields that differ: your version beside the bundle's. Choose Accept new to take the bundle's version, or Keep mine to keep yours. Keep mine stops it being listed until a later version changes that item again. Accepting a reminder email never switches it on.
