title: Managing roles

## Creating a custom role

On Settings, Users & Roles, press "+ New Role", give it a name and description, and tick the permissions it should grant. Custom roles belong to your organization only.

## Editing a role's permissions

Open a custom role from Users & Roles to change its name or its permissions. Built-in roles cannot be edited; to adjust one, create a custom role with the permissions you want and assign that instead.

## Changing someone's role

Edit the person on Users & Roles and choose a new role. This needs the Manage global platform settings permission, because giving someone a role is giving them its permissions. The change reaches them when they next sign in.
