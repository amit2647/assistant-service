## The Users & Roles page

Go to Settings, then Users & Roles, to see the people in your organization, the role each has and whether they are active, along with the list of roles.

## Built-in roles

OmniCore ships with these roles, which cannot be edited: Super Admin (unrestricted access to everything), Operations Manager (data integrity, workflows and reporting), Sales Manager (sales pipelines, assignments and team performance), Sales Representative (their assigned leads and customers), Marketing Manager (lead generation and marketing), Marketing Specialist (lead acquisition), Customer Success Manager (customer relationships and retention) and Customer Support Agent (customer service). To give someone a different mix of permissions, create a custom role.

## What a permission means

Each permission allows one kind of action on one area: for example "View lead records", "Create customer records", "Send emails to leads, customers and other recipients" or "Manage global platform settings". Opening a role shows every permission it grants.
