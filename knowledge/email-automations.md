## Email automations

Go to Settings, then Email Automations, to see emails that are sent automatically when something happens. Each automation pairs an event with the template to send and the email account it sends from. The list shows which account each one sends from.

## Events that can trigger an email

An automation can run when a lead is created, when a lead is converted into a customer, or when a customer is created.

## Automations are off until switched on

New automations start switched off, and nothing is sent until one is switched on. Once on, it sends real email from your connected mailbox every time its event happens.

## Choosing the account an automation sends from

Each automation has a "Send from" setting. While only one email account is connected, the organization default uses it. Once two or more accounts are connected, every automation must name one: an automation left on the default cannot be switched on, and the list marks it "Choose an account". Edit the automation and pick the account under Send from.
