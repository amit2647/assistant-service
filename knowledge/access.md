## Giving temporary access

Go to Settings, then Just-in-time Access, and press "+ Grant Access". Choose who it is for, the screens they should get, and for how long — from 15 minutes up to 24 hours. Access starts and stops immediately, without anyone signing out.

## Which screens can be granted

Temporary access can cover Leads, Customers, Services, Dashboard, Communication, Email settings and Users & Roles. Each screen grants viewing it; it does not grant creating, editing or deleting.

## Giving access to someone without an account

When granting access, choose "External person (no account)" and enter their email address. You get an invite link to send them; press "Copy link". Opening it gives them only the screens you granted, and nothing else, until the access expires.

## Revoking access

On Just-in-time Access, press Revoke next to an active grant. It stops working straight away, even for someone already using it.
