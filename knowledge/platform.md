title: How OmniCore is organized
---

## The main sections

OmniCore is a customer and lead management platform. The sidebar holds the main sections: Dashboard for figures, Leads for potential customers, Customers for accounts you work with, Services for what your organization sells, the Assistant, and Settings. You only see the sections your access includes.

## Why a screen or button is missing

What you can see and do is decided by your role, and each role is a set of permissions. If a section is not in your sidebar, or a button such as Delete is not shown, your role does not include that permission. An administrator can change your role, or give you temporary access to a screen for a limited time.

## Roles and permissions

A permission allows one thing, such as viewing leads or sending email. A role bundles permissions together, and every person has one role in the organization. OmniCore ships with built-in roles, and administrators can create custom roles of their own.

## Temporary access

Someone can be given access to a screen for a limited time without changing their role — or even without an account. This is called just-in-time access; it starts and stops immediately, and expires on its own.

## Your organization's data

Everything you see belongs to your organization. People in other organizations never see your leads, customers, settings or assistant conversations, and your assistant conversations are private to you.

## Signing out and sessions

Changes to your role reach you the next time you sign in. Temporary access is the exception: it applies straight away.
