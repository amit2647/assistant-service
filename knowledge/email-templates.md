## Email templates

Go to Settings, then Email Templates, to see reusable emails. Templates are what email automations send.

## Personalising a template

Templates can include placeholders that are filled in when the email is sent: {{lead.name}}, {{lead.company}}, {{customer.name}}, {{customer.company}} and {{organization.name}}. If a placeholder cannot be filled — for example {{lead.company}} for a lead with no company — it is left in the email as written, so the gap is noticed rather than silently blank.
