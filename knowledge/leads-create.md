## Adding a new lead

On the Leads page, press "+ Add Lead". Adding a lead has four steps:

1. Capture — the lead's details. Name and email are required.
2. Qualify — the status and a lead score between 0 and 100.
3. Services — the services the lead is interested in.
4. Review — check everything, then save.
