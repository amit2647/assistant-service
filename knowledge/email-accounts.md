## Connecting an email account

Go to Settings, then Email Accounts, to connect the mailbox that email is sent from and received into. Setup has four steps: Account (a name, the address and the provider — Gmail, Microsoft Outlook, Generic SMTP or Other), SMTP (the outgoing server, used to send), IMAP (the incoming server, used to pick up replies), and Review.

## Why replies show up on records

With IMAP set up, replies to email sent from OmniCore are collected from the mailbox and appear in the Communication section of the lead or customer they belong to.
