## Editing a lead

On the Leads page, press Edit on the lead's row, or Edit on its detail page. The same four steps as adding a lead open with the current details filled in. The status is chosen in the Qualify step.

## Converting a lead into a customer

A lead can be converted once its status is Qualified. On the Leads page, a Qualified lead has a Convert action in its row. Converting creates a customer from the lead, carries over the services the lead was interested in, and marks the lead as Converted.

If Convert is not shown, set the lead's status to Qualified first by editing it.
