title: Your profile
---

## Opening your profile

Click your name and avatar at the top right of any screen, then choose "My profile". It is also in Settings as "My Profile". Your profile shows your name, email, role, organization and when you joined.

## Changing your name

On My Profile, edit Name under Personal details and press "Save Changes". The new name shows in the header straight away.

## Changing your email address

On My Profile, edit Email under Personal details. Because your email is what you sign in with, a Current password field appears; enter your password and press "Save Changes". Sign in with the new address from then on. An address already used by someone else is refused.

## Changing your password

On My Profile, under Password, enter your current password, then the new one twice, and press "Change Password". The new password must be at least 8 characters and different from the current one.

## Changing your role

You cannot change your own role. Roles are set by an administrator on Users & Roles.

## Guest access

If you are signed in with temporary guest access, your profile is shown but cannot be changed.
